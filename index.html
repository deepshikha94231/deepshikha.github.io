01 · About

Building learning systems that make better decisions.

I am a PhD researcher at IIT Hyderabad working in machine learning and reinforcement learning. My current interests center on Deep Reinforcement Learning and problems involving sequential decisions under uncertainty.

I am also interested in statistical learning, bandit-style problems, and the use of reinforcement learning ideas in emerging applications such as language-model agents.
